What Changes
i.

Push-up graduates to floor

When 8 incline push-ups feel solid, drop the bench height. Eventually full floor push-ups for sets of 5, then 8. Earn the rep.

ii.

Pull-up negatives → band-assisted concentric

Loop a light band over the bar. Real reps up, not just lowering. 4 × 5 with the lightest band that gets you exactly 5.

iii.

Bulgarian split squats get loaded

From 25 lb DBs to 35–40 lb. The Wednesday hip work is where your butt and balance start showing real change.

iv.

Hip thrust gets heavy

Move to barbell if you haven't. 4 × 8 climbing each week. Glute strength is the engine of midsection definition.

v.

Saturday adds HIIT

If a weekend day has no tennis/golf/hike planned, run 8 × 250m on the rower hard with 90s rest. ~25 min total. Skip if the body says no.

Why The Diet Break Matters

Six months of cutting suppresses hormones, slows metabolism, burns through willpower. The break is structural, not a reward. Skip it and the final cut won't work.

Phase 3 Adjustments

Eat the food

Appetite may return as dose adjusts. Lean into it. Hormones recover. Strength jumps. This is the part of the year where you'll PR most lifts.

ii.

The Rules
01.

Fresh-cooked over leftovers

Histamines build in stored food. Cook portion-size, eat fresh. If you must save: freeze immediately, never refrigerate >24h.

02.

Protein at every meal — 30g floor

30+ g × 4 meals = 120g daily. Tirzepatide doesn't care if you lose fat or muscle. Protein + lifting decides which.

03.

Hydration is non-negotiable

2.5 L water minimum. The medication suppresses thirst. Set phone alarms if you have to. Electrolytes on lift days.

04.

Avoid the panel reactives

Eggs, cow whey, tree nuts, kidney beans, peanuts, yeast, wheat, tomatoes, hemp, pea. No vinegars (fermented). Distilled white vinegar OK. Coconut aminos for soy sauce sub.

05.

Veg volume saves the cut

2 cups minimum at lunch and dinner — spinach, zucchini, bell peppers, cucumbers, carrots. Fiber + bulk = full on fewer calories.

06.

The Truvani shake is insurance

Low-appetite days: this is how you hit protein without forcing food. Drink it cold, drink it slow.

07.

Coffee fine until 2 PM

Sleep is non-negotiable for fat loss and recovery. Late caffeine destroys deep sleep. ADHD meds aren't a reason to push it.

A · Bowl
Greek yogurt bowl with pumpkin seeds, berries & turkey sausage
07:30 · 6 min · serves 1
this is the breakfast you actually want to eat. Greek yogurt hits the satisfaction spot oatmeal can't — sweet without being heavy, dense protein in a small volume that works with appetite suppression. Pumpkin seeds bring the crunch plus zinc and magnesium that support recovery from the lifting. The turkey sausage on the side is protein insurance for the rest of the morning.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup Green Valley or Lactaid plain Greek lactose-free yogurt
  • 2 tbsp raw pumpkin seeds pepitas, unsalted
  • ½ cup mixed berries blueberries, raspberries
  • 2 Applegate Naturals turkey breakfast sausage links uncured, no nitrates
  • Pinch cinnamon
  • 1 tsp honey or maple, optional
Method
  1. Heat sausages in a dry skillet over medium, 4–5 minutes, turning, until browned through. (Applegate links are pre-cooked, so you're just heating and crisping the outside.)

  4. Eat the sausages alongside — or slice and stir into the yogurt for protein-dense bites.
Brand note: Applegate Naturals turkey breakfast sausage is the cleanest grocery-store option — uncured, no nitrates or preservatives, no added sugar. Wellshire and Niman Ranch also clean if Applegate is out. Avoid Jimmy Dean / Johnsonville / most store-brand — those have nitrates and fillers.
B · Savory
Salmon & sweet potato hash with spinach
07:30 · 18 min · serves 1
fresh salmon is gentle on digestion in a way smoked or aged fish isn't, the sweet potato gives steady energy that holds through a morning of meetings, and the spinach brings the iron and the green that makes a plate feel complete.

Ingredients
  • 4 oz fresh salmon, skin off, cubed ½"
  • 1 small sweet potato, peeled, diced ½"
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, black pepper, smoked paprika
  • Lemon wedge
Method
  1. Heat ½ tbsp olive oil in nonstick over medium-high. Add sweet potato + salt. Cook 8 min, stirring, until tender and edges caramelized.
  2. Push potatoes to one side. Add remaining oil + salmon cubes + paprika. Cook 3 min undisturbed, then toss 1 min — just cooked through.
  3. Add garlic + spinach. Toss until spinach wilts, ~1 min.
  4. Squeeze lemon over the top. Black pepper. Plate.
Best savory breakfast you'll have: high protein, anti-inflammatory, no fermented anything. Caramelized sweet potato is the move.

Lunch · 36–42g protein
A · Salmon
Lemon-dill salmon, smashed potatoes, spinach
12:30 · 35 min · serves 1
fresh salmon is gentle on digestion in a way smoked or aged fish isn't, the potatoes give you steady satisfying energy that answers end-of-day tiredness, and the big pile of spinach brings the fiber and the green that makes a plate feel complete.
Protein
40 g
Cal
520
Carbs
35 g
Fat
22 g
Ingredients
  • 5 oz fresh salmon fillet, skin on
  • 8 oz baby gold or red potatoes
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 lemon — zest + juice
  • 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • Salt, black pepper
Method
  1. Boil potatoes in salted water 12–15 min until fork-tender. Drain.
  2. Meanwhile, heat oven to 400°F. Drained potatoes on a sheet pan, smash each with a glass to ½" thick. Drizzle 1 tbsp olive oil, salt. Roast 15 min until crisp edges.
  3. Pat salmon dry. Salt + pepper. Heat remaining olive oil in nonstick over medium-high. Salmon skin-side-down 4 min — don't move it. Flip, 2 min more. Pull.
  4. Same pan, add garlic + spinach. Toss 1 min until wilted.
  5. Plate. Squeeze lemon over salmon. Sprinkle dill + lemon zest.
The 3-card classic. Crisp skin = pan must be hot, salmon must be dry. Don't peek for the first 4 minutes.
B · Shrimp
Garlic shrimp with rice & sautéed peppers
12:30 · 20 min · serves 1
shrimp is one of the most digestible proteins on your panel — no histamine load, quick cook, easy on the gut. White rice is a comfort carb that your body knows what to do with. The peppers bring sweetness and Vitamin C without any of the tomato family showing up.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup white rice, cooked
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cup zucchini, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Method
  1. Pat shrimp very dry — wet shrimp steams instead of sears.

  4. Off heat, squeeze lemon. Toss parsley.
  5. Plate rice, top with vegetables, shrimp over the top with all the garlic-oil from the pan.
Shrimp pro tip: if frozen, thaw in a bowl of cold water 15 min, then pat very dry with paper towels. Wet shrimp = rubber shrimp.

Garlic-ginger tofu fajita wrap
12:30 · 20 min · serves 1 · made night-before or AM-of
this is your plant-day wrap when you want something different from the salmon-or-turkey rotation. Fresh tofu is one of the cleanest plant proteins on your panel — non-fermented soy is well-tolerated where miso, tempeh, and soy sauce aren't. The garlic-ginger-coconut-aminos finish gives it real fajita flavor without anything fermented or aged.
Protein
37 g
Cal
480
Carbs
38 g
Fat
24 g
Ingredients
  • 6 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed see step 1
  • 1 large Siete cassava tortilla
  • 2 tbsp hummus or sunflower seed butter — see note below
  • ½ red bell pepper, sliced thin
  • ½ yellow or orange bell pepper, sliced thin
  • ¼ red onion, sliced thin
  • ½ cup baby spinach
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, split
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp coconut aminos
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch or tapioca starch for crispy edges
  • ½ tsp ground cumin · salt · black pepper
  • Fresh cilantro + lime wedge to finish
Method
  1. Press the tofu: wrap in a clean towel, set a heavy pan on top, 15 minutes. (Or use a tofu press if you have one.) Pressed tofu pan-fries crisp; soggy tofu steams and falls apart.
  2. While tofu presses, slice peppers and onion. Heat ½ tbsp olive oil in skillet over medium-high. Sauté peppers + onion + pinch of cumin + salt, 5 minutes until tender with charred edges. Remove to plate.
  3. Cube pressed tofu into ½" pieces. Toss in a bowl with cornstarch, remaining cumin, salt, pepper.
  4. Heat remaining ½ tbsp olive oil in the same pan over medium-high. Add tofu in single layer. Don't touch for 2 minutes. Flip, cook 2 min more. Repeat for all sides — about 8 minutes total — until golden and crispy.
  5. Add garlic and ginger to the pan, stir 30 seconds. Pour in coconut aminos. Toss tofu to coat. Off heat.
  6. Warm the tortilla 15 seconds in a dry pan to make it pliable.
  7. Spread hummus across center third of tortilla. Layer spinach, fajita veggies, then tofu. Scatter cilantro, squeeze lime.
  8. Fold sides in, roll tight from the bottom. Wrap in parchment if heading to the office.
A small note on the hummus: chickpeas are in the same legume family as pea, kidney bean, and pinto bean — all flagged on your panel. They weren't directly tested, so they may be fine — but if you start noticing low-grade reactions after this one, sunflower seed butter or a thick layer of mashed avocado swap in cleanly without changing the wrap much. Shelby would have a good read on whether to test-and-see or skip.

Pan-seared steak with roasted potatoes & greens
19:30 · 30 min · serves 1
lean red meat is body-friendly in a way that surprises people — high in iron, B-vitamins, and creatine, all of which support the lifting work you're doing. Smashed potatoes are pure comfort, the greens close the loop with fiber and folate.
Protein
42 g

540
Carbs

24 g
Ingredients
  • 5 oz sirloin or flank steak, ¾" thick
  • 8 oz baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 cups baby spinach (or zucchini)
  • 3 garlic cloves, 2 minced, 1 smashed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 sprig fresh rosemary or thyme
  • Flaky salt, black pepper
  • Lemon wedge
Method
  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, smashed garlic. Roast 25 min, flipping at 15 min.
  2. Pull steak from fridge — salt both sides — let sit 15 min while potatoes cook (room temp steak sears better).
  3. Heat remaining oil in cast iron or heavy pan over medium-high until shimmering. Lay steak in, don't move it. 3 min per side for medium-rare (130°F internal). Add rosemary + smashed garlic for last min — baste with the oil.

THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLES

Ten rules. Read once a month.

Programs change. The principles don't. When you're tempted to skip, to add, to chase a new method — come here first.

The Ten
01.

Protein protects what the cut threatens

On GLP-1s, your body will lose weight either way. Protein + lifting decide whether you lose fat or muscle. 120–130g daily, every day, no exceptions.

02.

Show up five times — even when small

The med makes you tired some days. A 30-minute version of the workout that gets done is worth more than the perfect one you skip. Log it either way.

03.

Progressive overload, always

Each week, add weight, reps, or quality on the priority lifts. The Log tab tells you what last week looked like. Beat it by something.

04.

Hips, core, balance every week

Wednesday is the dedicated day. But Bulgarian splits, dead bugs, Pallof presses, and single-leg work weave through every other day too. The midsection follows.

05.

Hydration is non-negotiable

Tirzepatide suppresses thirst. Drink 2.5L water minimum. Add electrolytes on lift days. Headaches and fatigue are usually dehydration, not effort.

06.

Fresh food, not stored

Histamine builds in storage. Cook portion-size. Eat within 24h if you must save. Freeze immediately for longer.

07.

Sleep first, supplements last

7+ hours minimum. Caffeine cutoff 2 PM — yes, even on stimulant days. Creatine 5g daily is the only supplement worth the line item.

08.

The scale lies weekly, tells truth monthly

Weigh daily, look at the 7-day average. Sodium, period, glycogen, water — they all add 1–4 lb noise. The 4-week trend is the real signal.

09.

Photos beat the scale

Photos every 4 weeks, same morning, same light, same suit. Waist-tape too. These tell the truth when the scale doesn't.

10.

Consistency outscores perfection

Four good workouts a week for 50 weeks beats five perfect workouts for 12. 80% adherence beats 100% for two weeks then quitting.
