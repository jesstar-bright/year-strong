Deep sage.

The cool colour that doesn't fight the pink. It's a green with warmth in it — closer to a eucalyptus than a forest — so it sits on warm paper without the clash you get from blue. Pink stays the only action colour; sage is for information.

Done states vs. actions

Sage = already happened. Pink = do it now. That single rule is the whole reason to add a third colour.

The mark on sage

All four now have equal padding on every side — the paw no longer touches the edge. Sage makes a good alternate icon or splash screen, but ink stays the primary.

Where it must not go

Never on a primary button. One action colour, and it's pink. A sage CTA next to a pink CTA makes the user pick between two equal-looking choices.

Never as body text on paper. Deep sage passes contrast, but coloured paragraphs read as links. Body copy is ink.

Never more than one sage block per screen. It's a punctuation mark, not a surface.
