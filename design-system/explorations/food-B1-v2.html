Recipe B1 · Monday, from Sunday's steak

Steak & eggs with charred
red pepper chimichurri

07:30 · 12 min · serves 1 · one pan high iron10 min

The steak is already cooked and sitting in the fridge, so this is a reheat and two eggs. Haem iron from beef plus the iron in the yolks, landing next to a whole charred red pepper — 150 mg of vitamin C in the same pan. It is the highest-absorption breakfast in the file and it takes twelve minutes.

Batch chain · consumes

Dinner D1's steak and its potatoes. Cook 1 lb sirloin Sunday and boil extra potatoes; 4 oz steak plus a handful of potatoes come here Monday morning, and the last 4 oz goes to Lunch L1.

Iron rule

Egg iron is non-haem and yolk phosvitin binds it hard, so the pepper is the working ingredient, not a garnish. Coffee before this meal is free; coffee within an hour after it costs you ~39%.

Morck, Lynch & Cook 1983, AJCN 37:416

Ingredients

  • 4 oz cooked sirloin, sliced thin across the grain
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 whole red bell pepper, quartered ~150 mg vit C
  • ¼ cup flat-leaf parsley, chopped fine
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 2 tsp olive oil + 1 tsp for the pan
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, chilli flakes, salt
  • 150 g cooled boiled potatoes, halved

Method

  1. Dry skillet on high. Char the pepper quarters skin-down 4 min until blackened in patches. Pull out and slice.
  2. Chimichurri: parsley, garlic, vinegar, olive oil, chilli, salt. Stir in a mug. Thirty seconds.
  3. Same pan, 1 tsp oil, medium-high. Potatoes cut-side down, 4 min undisturbed, until they take colour.
  4. Lay the sliced steak in for 45 seconds a side — you are warming it, not cooking it again.
  5. Push aside, crack the eggs in, lid on 2 min for set whites and runny yolks.
  6. Plate: peppers, steak, eggs. Chimichurri over everything.
Why the vinegar earns its place. Vinegar with a carbohydrate meal lowers the glucose and insulin response — pooled standardised effects of −0.60 and −1.30. Honest limit: it blunts the spike, it does not move fasting insulin. Here the acid is already in the sauce, so it is free.
