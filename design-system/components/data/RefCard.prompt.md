Use `RefCard` for anything backed by a study. Sage signals "this is why", not "do this".

```jsx
<RefCard title="Iron rule" cite="Morck, Lynch & Cook 1983, AJCN 37:416">
  Coffee an hour before the meal is free. An hour after costs you about 39%.
</RefCard>
```

Never put a button inside one. One per screen.
