# Year Strong — Design System

**Year Strong** is a twelve-month strength-and-nutrition program built as a personal iOS-style app. Five lift days a week, three 40g-protein meals a day, and a research library behind every claim. It tracks sets, macros, phases and notes for one person doing one hard thing for a year.

This repository is the brand and interface system for that app.

## The one rule

**Pink means do it now. Sage means it already happened.** Everything else is ink on warm paper. That single distinction carries the whole system — it is why the CTA is pink and the completed checkbox is sage, why the Reference cards are sage and the Log button is not.

## Content fundamentals

The app talks like a good coach who is slightly bored of being asked. **Dry, direct, never encouraging for its own sake.**

- **Person.** Second person, always. *"Your job is to lift consistently."* Never "we", never "let's".
- **Casing.** Screen titles are sentence case. Primary buttons are ALL CAPS verbs — `LOG SET`, `SAVE SET`, `START THE YEAR`. Eyebrows and metadata are mono caps — `DAY G · SUNDAY`, `0 OF 8 DONE`.
- **The italic aside.** Exactly one italic line per screen, and it is the only sentence that is not data: *"No load, no score — go to the first honest resistance and breathe there."* If a screen has two, cut one.
- **Honesty about evidence.** Every claim carries its citation and, where the science is thin, says so: *"One trial, never replicated at that size."* This is the most distinctive thing about the product's voice — do not smooth it out.
- **Numbers do the talking.** `4 × 8`, `120 g`, `5.1 mg`, `17 days`. Set in Space Mono so they scan.
- **Never:** emoji, exclamation marks, "crush it", "you've got this", streak-shaming, or any praise the user did not earn.
- **Sample microcopy:** *"Don't push PRs — log what you can, come back next week."* · *"The scale lies weekly, tells truth monthly."* · *"Coffee after breakfast, not with it."* · *"set logged"* (toast) · *"Every logged set goes. There is no undo."* (alert)

## Visual foundations

### Colour
`--pink #FF2E93` is the action colour and appears **once per screen**. Text-weight pink is `--pink-ink #C4156A` (4.9:1 on paper — the bright pink fails as text and is fill-only). `--sage #2F5D50` is the information colour: reference cards, completed checkboxes, done stats, the Notes tab. Everything else is `--ink #1A1A18` on `--paper #F7F5F0`.

Paper is **warm-neutral, not cream**. Backgrounds are never `#FFF`, text is never `#000`, and there are no gradients anywhere in the system.

**Dark mode** is a warm near-black (`#141413`), and the CTA softens from hot pink to `--pink-soft #FFBFDC` — hot pink at 6am in a dark gym is too much. Every pairing in both modes passes WCAG AA.

### Typography
| Role | Family | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display `--font-display` | **Gluten** 900 | Screen titles, big numbers, button labels |
| Body `--font-body` | **Nunito** 400–800 | Everything you read. 17px at 1.62 |
| Label `--font-mono` | **Space Mono** 700 | Everything you scan: days, counts, reps, macros, citations |

All three are Google Fonts substitutions; no licensed faces exist for this brand. Handwriting was tested and rejected — it failed on the dense reference pages.

### Space and touch
4→64px scale. `--screen-x` (24px) is the phone gutter. iOS metrics are explicit and non-negotiable: **44px minimum tap target**, 56px list and set rows, 62px primary CTA, 24px home-indicator inset.

### Borders, radii, shadows
Everything is outlined — `--stroke-pen` **2.5px** is the default border on every card, button and table head. Corners: 10px fields, 16px lists, 20px cards, 22px sheets, pill buttons.

The house shadow is a **hard offset with zero blur** — `4px 5px 0` in ink. It appears on cards, buttons, toasts and menus. There is exactly one soft shadow, `--shadow-sheet`, for the bottom sheet. In dark mode the shadow inverts to bone.

### Interaction
- **Hover** lifts: `translate(-1px,-2px)`, shadow grows.
- **Press** sits down onto the shadow: `translate(3px,4px)`, shadow to zero. This is the signature gesture — never substitute a scale-down.
- **Focus** is a 3px pink-ink ring at 2px offset.
- **Selected** is a pink fill (chips, day strip) or a pink inset rule (tabs).
- **Done** is sage, always.

### Motion
Default easing is `--ease-bounce`; sheets use `--ease-ios`. Durations 90 / 160 / **240** / 380ms. Sheets rise, alerts and toasts pop with a slight scale. Progress never animates on load — it should already be true when the screen appears. All motion collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Transparency and blur
Two places only: the sheet/alert veil (42% ink + 12px backdrop blur) and nothing else. No frosted panels, no translucent cards.

## Iconography

⚠️ **The app currently uses almost no icons, and that is deliberate.** Navigation is mono-caps text labels; controls are `−`/`+`, `‹`, `›`, `✓`, `⌕`, `⋯`. This keeps the interface readable at a glance mid-set and avoids a glyph vocabulary nobody has learned.

- **Unicode marks in use:** `✓` (checklist, menu selection), `‹` `›` (back, row chevron), `−` `+` (stepper), `⌕` (search), `⋯` (more), `→` (movement sequences in exercise copy).
- **If a real icon set is ever needed**, use [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) at 18–20px, `currentColor`, 2px stroke, never filled. This would be a substitution — flag it if you add one.
- **Emoji: never.**
- The only illustration in the system is the tiger mark.

## The mark

A crouching tiger whose tail sweeps up and becomes the **Y**, ending in a curl over where the **S** falls. Generated by the client, recoloured and cleaned here.

`assets/logo/` — `icon-sage-render.png` (**primary**, App Store and home screen), `icon-ink-render.png`, `icon-paper-render.png`, all 1024px squircle-masked with equal padding; plus `mark-pink-render.png`, `mark-ink.png`, `mark-paper.png` on transparent backgrounds.

⚠️ **These are raster**, recoloured from a generated JPEG. Edges carry softness and the stripes have a gradient baked in. Fine for the app and the store listing — **get it traced to vector before print, embroidery or any use above 1024px.**
